# UseTally Business Clarity Kit

**A commercial Excel-based business record management and bookkeeping product for small businesses.**

The UseTally Business Clarity Kit helps business owners organise business records in one workbook. This repository is a **public product case study** documenting the problem, product structure, selected capabilities, testing approach, and lessons from building the workbook.

> **Commercial product notice:** The complete workbook is sold commercially and is intentionally not included in this repository. No sample workbook is provided here.

## The problem

Small businesses can struggle with incomplete, inconsistent, or scattered records. This makes it harder to understand business performance, manage day-to-day operations, make informed decisions, and present reliable information when seeking financing opportunities.

The kit explores how a structured Excel workbook can make record-keeping more organised and accessible.

## Product scope

The workbook brings together business information and operational record-keeping, including:

- Business Information
- Products
- Inventory
- Suppliers
- Customers
- Sales
- Purchases
- Expenses
- Dashboard and financial summaries

See [`documentation/features.md`](documentation/features.md) for a module-by-module overview.

## Key product capabilities

- **Automated IDs across applicable entry modules:** Modules that require records use automatic ID generation, including Customers, Sales, Expenses, and other applicable entry modules. This reduces the need for users to create record identifiers manually.
- Automated SKU generation for products.
- Formula-driven calculations and linked summaries.
- Dashboard KPIs and business summaries.
- Dropdown lists and data validation to guide consistent data entry.
- Worksheet protection intended to help preserve workbook structure and formulas.
- Organised records for sales, expenses, inventory, purchases, customers, and suppliers.

Feature details should be read alongside the actual workbook version. This repository documents the product; it does not replace the workbook or its user guide.

## Product-building focus

This case study highlights the process of turning small-business record-keeping needs into a structured product: defining modules and fields, designing connected tables, implementing spreadsheet logic, improving data-entry consistency, protecting formulas, and testing common workflows.
